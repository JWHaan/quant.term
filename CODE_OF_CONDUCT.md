# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want `quant.term` to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project. Participation should be free from harassment regardless of identity, background, experience level, nationality, appearance, disability, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal worth.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Credit sources and other contributors.
- Respect privacy and do not publish sensitive information.
- Prioritize user safety and honest data presentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly misleading technical or financial claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. If it is safe to do so, report repository-specific conduct through an issue without including sensitive personal information. For harassment, threats, doxxing, or other platform-level abuse, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and a proportionate path to correction.
